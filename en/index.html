About KU Padel

KU Padel was founded in the winter of 2022. It is a student-run association that brings students together around padel across years and fields of study. Membership is free, and our aim is to give students at the University of Copenhagen the best possible conditions for playing padel, along with the perks that come with it.

Our vision is to build a large community centred on the sport and, over time, to offer events, experiences and sports networking.

Follow us on Instagram and Facebook.
